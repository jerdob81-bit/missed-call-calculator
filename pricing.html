The part the sticker price hides

Phone and texting are billed by usage on top of the plan. HighLevel's help center lists a US local number at $1.15 a month, US texts at $0.00747 per segment (sent or received), outbound calls at $0.0166 per minute and inbound calls at $0.01165 per minute, plus carrier fees. US texting also needs A2P 10DLC registration: HighLevel lists a one-time fee of $22.50 for sole proprietor and low-volume standard brands and a monthly campaign fee of $1.50 to $10. Your monthly total depends on how many people you call and text. Rates as listed September 2026; check HighLevel for current pricing.

How to judge the 14-day trial

  1. On day 1, start A2P 10DLC texting registration. Approval is not instant, and texts to US numbers may not deliver until it is approved.
  2. Set up your HighLevel number or call routing, turn on Missed Call Text Back, and write the message and booking link.
  3. Once registration is approved, call your own business number, let it ring out, and confirm the text arrives.
  4. For the rest of the trial, count missed calls, text-backs delivered, replies, and jobs booked from those replies.
  5. Before day 14, decide whether to keep the account. Billing starts automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel. If registration was not approved in time to test, count that as an untested trial, not a failed one, and decide whether to pay to keep testing.
